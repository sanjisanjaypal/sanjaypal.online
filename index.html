About

Create products your users will want to use.

I’m Sanjay Pal, a UX designer passionate about creating intuitive, human-centred digital experiences that bring together user needs, business goals and technology.

Over the past 22+ years, I’ve worked across 500+ websites, 50+ mobile applications, and numerous enterprise platforms, web applications and digital products. My work spans UX strategy, product design, interaction design, user research, design systems and visual storytelling. I enjoy transforming complex problems into simple, meaningful experiences that make a real difference to people and businesses.

As a design leader, I believe great design goes beyond aesthetics. It’s about understanding people, challenging assumptions, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and finding the right balance between usability, technical feasibility and business impact. I enjoy mentoring designers, building collaborative teams, and exploring how emerging technologies, particularly AI, can shape the next generation of digital experiences.

My approach to design is rooted in empathy, curiosity and continuous learning. Whether I’m shaping a digital product, refining an enterprise experience, or exploring new design possibilities with AI, I strive to make technology feel more intuitive, accessible and human.

Beyond design, I have a creative side that extends into music and visual storytelling. I enjoy exploring new ideas and bringing them to life through different creative mediums, always looking for inspiration in the world around me.
